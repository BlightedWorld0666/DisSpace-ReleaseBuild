# DisSpace

**Your music, your presence, your stream.**

DisSpace is a free Windows app that brings together customizable Discord Rich Presence, synced lyrics, and music overlays for OBS and Streamlabs.

### Make it yours

- **Music presence:** Share what you’re listening to on Discord with song details, album artwork, and lyrics.
- **Custom statuses:** Build your own presence with custom text, images, and buttons.
- **Stream overlays:** Display your current song, artwork, playback progress, and synced lyrics on stream.
- **Audio-reactive visuals:** Add effects that respond specifically to Spotify’s audio.
- **Personalized appearance:** Choose themes and customize your overlay’s layout, visualizer, and background effects.
- **Spotify connection:** Optionally connect your account for directly matched track information and album artwork.

DisSpace runs locally on your PC and stays accessible from the system tray. Its browser-source overlays work with OBS and Streamlabs without requiring a streaming-software login.

Spotify Canvas video support is experimental, with album artwork used when a video is unavailable.
